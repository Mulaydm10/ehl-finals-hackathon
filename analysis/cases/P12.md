## 5. € per lever (exact)
How `quality` works in C: committed units × ppm × (€275 − min(chargeback, €275)). So:
- **chargeback ≥ €300 sets the quality cost to 0 whatever the ppm**; at €150 each defect still costs us €125.
- ppm 4,118 → 300 at chargeback €150 saves only **€4.3k (0.46% of price)**.
- To the supplier, a chargeback costs real ppm × € per complaint: €300 at ~4,100 ppm ≈ **€1.24/pc (1.2%)**, more than it

## 6. Recommendation

**Target:** €97.00, ppm ≤ 800, chargeback €150, lead 23 (0.93). **Good:** ≤ €98.50 (0.77). **Walk-away:** anything

## What generalises (hidden quality cases)
- Compute the quality term before arguing about it: **if cost_per_defect ≤ a chargeback option, that chargeback zeroes the
  quality cost** and the ppm commitment no longer matters in C.
- Without a ppm must-have, the quality data is leverage for **price**, not a goal in itself.
- When the alternative is "today's terms continue", any sheet must beat C_alt: check it before every accept.
