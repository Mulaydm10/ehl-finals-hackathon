## What generalises (hidden capacity / secure_supply cases)
- **Volume and lead dominate**: with a short horizon and €1–2k per day of lead, 1% of price is noise. Rank levers by ΔC first.
- **Price at should-cost + sustainable margin = no price room**: the relationship charge makes C flat or rising below it.
- **Cash-wanting supplier + negotiable payment days**: pay faster for allocation, not for price.
