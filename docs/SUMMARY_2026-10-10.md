## The ten things that matter most
1. **Know the supplier's engine and its final trigger.** Mirror (5/10), holder (P03, P11), floor-stop (P04), one-shot (P10),
   auction (P05). Suppliers go final at a fixed message count (89% of 353 replies); holders after 1-3 holds.
2. **Structure beats arguments.** Lead with the structure it prices best for us; hold it; adopt a term it refuses twice; at a
   floor-stop supplier pick the TERM from memory (P04 45 d = 0.874 vs 14 d = 0.815).
3. **One new verified fact per round, never an empty round.** A code-priced supplier cuts per accepted fact; an empty round
   ("Which figures is that based on?") triggers its final. Weak facts wait for a hold - but never leave a round empty.
4. **Only claims whose check you can reproduce.** One number per claim sentence, no thousands separator; a contradicted claim
   costs trust and closes the game early. Never cite a rising index; one index claim per e-mail.
5. **Pace by engine.** Mirrors: 30% of the gap per round (P07 0.75 -> 0.81, P02 0.51 -> 0.85). Holders: pack 2 facts per round.
   Patient / cash-short / hedged suppliers: small steps.
6. **Never push after final_offer** (~1.1 points lost live); accept only its PRIMARY sheet. Walk guard only when it stalls,
   never on template sentences it writes every time.
7. **Supplier memory.** Every game rebuilds `datasets/H_supplier_memory.json` (prices agreed / refused / finals / named floors
   per structure); probes, term choice and the model's prompt read it. It lets every game build on the previous ones.
8. **Find the floor, then go just under it before its final.** Ask the floor (it answers); grid suppliers hide the floor between
   price-grid points (P11 agreed to 44.95 while quoting 45.00 -> 0.80); a one-cent close BEFORE the final costs nothing.
   Some stops are hard (P04: one cent under refused on 45 d and 90 d -> 0.874 is its ceiling).
9. **Play the person, honestly.** Pay in its currency (P11: owning our 96%-late payments, no chargebacks, call-off plan);
   name the fear we remove, never accuse. Hard-ball only with true facts (our own escalation letter, a real second source).
   No prompt injection, no fabricated data - the arena understands manipulation and wrong figures cost trust.
10. **Rules conflict - test every change against every case.** Most of today's P11 losses were our own rules colliding
    (probe vs facts-left, weak-facts gate vs empty round, structure lock vs floor pair, template line vs walk guard,
    premium extrapolation). Offline Qwen -> offline Astra -> replays of real games -> live; judge coin-flip cases on 2+ games.
